Mirror and control your Android phone from Windows over USB or local Wi-Fi. Record the screen, transfer files, and choose the frame rate for everyday use or Premium Game Mode.

## Install and buy

1. Download the Public Portable ZIP from the latest release, extract the whole archive, and run **Mirror Easy for Android.exe** on Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11, x64.
2. Try Premium for seven days from your first launch. Basic mirroring remains available after the trial.
3. To keep Premium, open **Settings → Purchase and license**. Checkout runs through **Polar** in your browser; the app activates the license after verified payment. You can also enter an existing direct-sale key. Downloading the ZIP itself is not a purchase.

The portable executable is currently unsigned and updates are manual: return to [Releases](https://github.com/volant-ger/Mirror-Easy-for-Android-Downloads/releases) for a newer archive. The Microsoft Store edition is separately installed and updated through Microsoft Store; Store purchases belong to that edition. Keep the bundled files together. License notices and the privacy policy are included in the archive. No Mirror Easy account is required.
